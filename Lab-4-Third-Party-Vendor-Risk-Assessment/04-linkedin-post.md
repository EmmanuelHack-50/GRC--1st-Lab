# LinkedIn Post — Lab 4

For my 4th Cybersecurity GRC lab, I explored **third-party vendor risk assessment** through a supply-chain security scenario.

I created a vendor questionnaire, scored responses across 12 security areas, and assessed three fictional EU vendors. The exercise helped me see how control gaps—such as weak MFA, limited recovery testing, and poor subcontractor visibility—can translate into business and supply-chain risk.

My key takeaway: a questionnaire score is only a starting point. Good vendor risk management also means validating evidence, agreeing remediation actions, and making risk-based decisions with the business.

I’ve documented the assessment, scoring, risk register, and treatment plan in my GitHub portfolio.
