## C. Disposition guidance

- **Pass:** The assessment is sufficiently complete, consistent and evidence-supported for its intended use.
- **Pass with observations:** No material issue prevents use, but improvements should be tracked.
- **Rework required:** A material gap affects confidence in a rating, control conclusion or treatment decision.
- **Escalate:** A significant risk, unresolved disagreement, potential conflict of interest or urgent governance issue needs management attention.

## D. Reviewer principles

1. Be objective and evidence-led.
2. Review the assessment against the same criteria for each assessor.
3. Distinguish a missing document from proof that a control is absent.
4. Do not assume a policy proves that a control operates effectively.
5. Do not change a risk rating solely to make the result look better or worse.
6. Give assessors a fair opportunity to explain evidence and context.
7. Record disagreements and unresolved limitations transparently.
8. Confirm closure using evidence, not verbal assurance alone.
9. Keep risk ownership with the designated risk owner.
10. Use recurring findings to improve the process, not just individual records.

## E. Suggested workflow status

`Selected → In Review → Clarification Requested → Action Agreed → Evidence Submitted → QA Validation → Closed / Reopened / Escalated`

**Minimum workflow fields:** assessment ID, version, service, assessor, service owner, reviewer, review date, criterion, finding severity, action owner, due date, status, response, closure evidence and final disposition.
